# Stage 3: level-aware, multi-timeframe models on the board's own decisions: pre-registration (2026-09-29)

**The operator's claims.**

1. The "structure" models failed because they never saw support and
   resistance zones on several timeframes.
2. They never saw the technicals a trader watches: EMAs, Bollinger bands,
   trend on every timeframe, and the day-trading toolkit.
3. They were trained with the wrong hyperparameters, learning rates and
   epochs.

The research note
[feature-research-2026-09-29.md](feature-research-2026-09-29.md) (126
sources) checked each claim against the code. It is right about the
2026-09-27/28 sequence models:

- no overnight gaps in the sequence;
- no relative volume;
- no price level of any kind;
- a 29-bar receptive field;
- channel averaging in PatchTST;
- no slot normalization;
- 20 fixed epochs at 1e-3, with no validation, no early stopping and one
  seed.

It is not right about the whole record. The September tree models and a
chart-image CNN were given the 33 daily technicals (EMA 9/21/50/200, SMA200,
weekly EMAs, 52-week high and low, MACD, candles) and the 11 swing-level
features. They reached a rank IC of 0.010-0.019 (t < 1.2). No model on this
book has yet been given gap-aware, level-relative, relative-volume,
multi-timeframe inputs *and* been trained properly.

This note fixes, before any stage-3 code exists, everything the test will
use:

- the two questions and the decision each one changes;
- the rows;
- every input, with the moment it is known live;
- the labels;
- the three model families, with their grids, epochs, learning rates,
  early stopping and seeds;
- the walk-forward;
- the decision tests, the kill criteria and the trial count.

The commit that adds this note comes before the commit that adds any
`stage3_*` module. Nothing below is tuned after the run. Any change is a
new, counted trial.

## The two questions

### T-I: now or the close (the board's BUY, SELL and TRIM moment)

For name i on session s at the close of fifteen-minute slot k (k = 0 is the
09:30-09:45 bar):

    y(i, s, k) = 10^4 · ln( P_close(i, s) / C_k(i, s) )      [bp]

`P_close` is the official close, the closing auction's first print, or the
last regular bar's close where the cube has no auction row
(`fill_timing._official_close`). `C_k` is bar k's close. Rows run over
k = 0..23, the bars closing 09:45 to 15:30. At 15:30 the board enters its
close window, and every later order goes to the close.

A forecast ŷ above zero says that buying now beats waiting for the close. A
forecast below zero says that selling now beats waiting. One model serves
buys and sells, because both compare the same two prices. T-I contains the
research note's separate targets:

- **T-E1 and T-X1** (take the 1% dip, or the 1% pop, or wait) are T-I read
  at the trigger slot.
- **T-E3** (15:30 or the close auction) is T-I at k = 23, where the SPY
  half-hour inputs (I14) are defined.

T-S3 (the order in which the redeploy leg funds names) is deferred and not
counted.

The decisions, as two fill conventions added to
`backend/market/fill_timing.py`. The engine is extended, not forked, and
every existing convention's prices stay byte-identical.

- **`<family>_filter`.** This keeps the board's trigger and lets the model
  veto it.
  - Buy: at the first bar whose close is at or below open × 0.99 (slot k*,
    as `dip_or_close`), fill at `C_k*` if ŷ(k*) > 0, else at the official
    close. If no bar triggers, fill at the close.
  - Sell: at the first bar whose close is at or above open × 1.01, fill at
    `C_k*` if ŷ(k*) < 0, else at the close.
  - A trigger at k* = 24 or 25 (after 15:30) has no forecast and fills as
    `dip_or_close` does. It is counted.
- **`<family>_free`.** The model picks the moment.
  - Buy: fill at `C_k` for the first k in 0..23 with ŷ(k) > 0, else at the
    official close.
  - Sell: fill at the first k with ŷ(k) < 0, else at the close.

`<family>` is `lgbm` (M1) or `seq` (M3). An order whose (name, session) has
no forecast fills as `dip_or_close` and is counted. This happens before the
first out-of-sample block, or where the dataset has no row.

### T-S1: which graded names to hold for the next twenty sessions

For name i decided on at session t's close:

    r(i, t) = ln( Õ(i, t+21) / Õ(i, t+1) ) − mean over the date's eligible names of the same
    y(i, t) = rank-Gauss of r(i, t) across the date's eligible names: Φ⁻¹((rank − 0.5)/n)

Õ is the dividend-adjusted open, `open × adj_close / close` on the panel.
The row fills at t+1's open and is held for twenty sessions: the
scorecard's plain execution and the `/4` reset clock. Rows whose t+21 runs
past the panel keep a NaN label. They are predicted, but not trained on.

The decision is an overlay on the live executor as it runs tonight. The
control is `ew-redeploy`: `market_pit_scorecard._live_options` plus
`midcycle_redeploy=True`, exactly `profit_taking.control_options`. The
overlay wraps `policy_v4.allocator(mask)`, following the
`profit_taking.ProfitTaking` pattern:

- Each time the allocator is called, take the A/A+ names with a positive
  policy target and a finite forecast. If there are n ≥ 5 of them, the
  ceil(0.1 · n) with the lowest ŷ get a zero target.
- The remaining targets are recomputed as the policy's equal weight over
  the reduced set, under the same 20% cap.
- A name with no forecast keeps its policy target.
- There are no mid-cycle trims: a dropped name leaves at the next reset,
  and the redeploy leg buys toward the reduced targets.

## Rows

- **Names and membership.**
  - The book's names that have a SIP cube: 94 names, plus SPY, QQQ, SMH and
    IGV as market inputs.
  - Point-in-time membership through `market_session_anatomy.membership_mask`,
    as in stage 2.
  - The name must carry a desk grade on the decision date.
  - The survivorship limit of every study on this book applies unchanged.
    The cubes hold today's names, so a name that left the index before
    2026 is absent. It is stated, not corrected.
- **T-I rows.**
  - Session s must be a complete cube session: 26 slots and a prior close.
  - The name must be eligible and graded at t, the panel session before s.
  - Rows cover k = 0..23.
  - About 83,000 name-sessions, so about 2.0 million rows.
- **T-S1 rows.** Every eligible, graded (i, t) on the panel from the first
  cube session, about 83,000.
- **Windows.**
  - 2016-2023 is the choosing window and 2024-2026 is reported. The last
    session of 2026 used is the last one with labels.
  - The decision tests judge each model from its first out-of-sample
    session, about 2018-02 after the 500-session warm-up. Sessions before
    then carry no forecast and would only dilute the paired difference.
  - Model decisions are therefore judged on 2018-02..2023-12, with
    2016-2023 printed beside it.

### Daily block

Computed from the desk's adjusted daily panel (`Panel`). Highs and lows are
scaled by `adj_close / close`, as `levels.py` does.

  session before s.
- **Volatility units.** σ20 is the 20-session standard deviation of daily

  the close.

### Intraday block (T-I rows)

**The rule of the moment.** A feature at the close of slot k uses:

- the complete SIP bars 0..k−1: open, high, low, close and volume;
- the close `C_k` of bar k, and nothing else of bar k.

This is what the board can know live. Prices arrive in real time from
IEX. Consolidated bars arrive 15 minutes late on the free plan
(AGENTS.md, 2026-09-28), and IEX carries about 2.5% of volume. A test
tampers with bar k's high, low and volume and every field of bars > k,
and asserts that row k is unchanged.

**Units.**

- **σ_slot(j).** The root mean square of the name's bar-j return over the
  prior 60 cube sessions. r_0 = ln(C_0/O_0), and r_j = ln(C_j/C_{j−1}).
- **σ_open→k.** The standard deviation of ln(C_k/O_0) at the same k over
  the prior 60 sessions.
- **σ15.** The standard deviation of all 15-minute bar returns over the
  prior 60 sessions.
- **ATR14.** The daily ATR at t = s−1.

**Columns.**

| ID | Columns |
|---|---|
| I00 | slot k; minutes to the close |
| I01 | gap g = ln(O_0 / prior close), raw and ÷ the 60-session standard deviation of gaps |
| I02 | ln(C_k/O_0), raw and ÷ σ_open→k |
| I03 | z_k, z_{k−1}, z_{k−2}, where z_j = r_j/σ_slot(j) |
| I04 | opening range over m = 1 and m = 2 bars, defined from k ≥ m: position (C_k − OR_lo)/(OR_hi − OR_lo); width ln(OR_hi/OR_lo) ÷ ATR14; above-OR_hi and below-OR_lo flags |
| I05 | VWAP_{k−1} = Σ_{j<k} TP_j·V_j / Σ_{j<k} V_j with TP = (H+L+C)/3; ln(C_k/VWAP_{k−1}) ÷ σ_open→k; share of bars j < k whose close was above the VWAP through j; VWAP crosses through k−1 (defined from k ≥ 1) |
| I06 | RVOL = ln(cumulative volume through k−1 / the mean of the same over the prior 20 sessions); opening RVOL (bar 0 against its 20-session mean) from k ≥ 1 |
| I07 | ln(C_k/PDH), ln(C_k/PDL), ln(C_k/PDC) ÷ ATR14; flags for a prior-day low or high already broken (by lows or highs of bars < k, or by C_k) |
| I08 | floor pivots P, R1, S1, R2, S2 of the prior session: ln(C_k/level) ÷ ATR14 |
| I09 | Camarilla H3, L3, H4, L4: the same |
| I10 | ln(min(L_{j<k}, C_k)/O_0) and ln(max(H_{j<k}, C_k)/O_0), ÷ σ_open→k; the position of C_k in that range |
| I11 | 15-minute EMA9 and EMA21 over the name's consecutive cube closes: ln(C_k/EMA) ÷ σ15; the 3-bar slope of EMA9 ÷ σ15; EMA9 crosses today; 15-minute RSI(14) |
| I12 | Σ_{j≤k} z_j²; the share of that sum from negative returns; max \|z_j\| |
| I13 | ln(C_k/O_0) − β60 · the same for the side ETF, and for SPY, ÷ σ_open→k |
| I14 | SPY r_1 = ln(SPY C_1 / SPY prior close) from k ≥ 1; SPY since open; SPY ln(C_k/VWAP_{k−1}); SPY r_12 = ln(C_23/C_21) at k = 23 only; QQQ and side ETF since open |
| I15 | across the session's eligible names at slot k: share above their prior close, share above their VWAP_{k−1}, mean sign of r_k |
| IS | intraday S/R from `sr_levels.session_levels` at slot k (details below) |
| IT | 15-minute trend sign (C above EMA21 with EMA9 above EMA21 = +1, both reversed = −1, else 0); 60-minute trend sign, the same on hourly closes of completed hours; alignment over 15m, 60m, daily, weekly and monthly (−5..5) |

The prior-day levels come from the panel at t, moved to s's raw basis by
the cube's prior close: ratio = `prior_close` / panel close at t.

IS gives, at slot k: the nearest support below C_k and the nearest
resistance above, from all 22 levels in force, as distance ÷ ATR14; the
confluence at each; an inside-zone flag; and the nearest support's family
group (intraday, swing, moving average, range, volume node). The opening
range is in force from slot 2 and the VWAP runs through bar k−1.

The daily block at t = s−1 is joined to every T-I row.

### Data that cannot be built, excluded

- I17: dealer gamma (no options history).
- X04: short interest.
- Pre-market bars.
- Analyst coverage and news other than EDGAR.
- A bar-level VWAP or trade counts. The store keeps OHLCV, so the VWAP is
  the typical-price proxy.
- I18, the closing auction, is a label only.

### Normalization

- **T-I (a per-order, time-series decision).** Columns stay in the
  volatility units above.
- **T-S1 (a cross-sectional decision).** Every column that varies across
  names is replaced by its per-date rank-Gauss over the date's eligible
  names. Date-level columns stay raw: regime, VIX, calendar, breadth and
  I14-style market inputs.
- **M1 (trees).** The columns are used as they are, with NaN left as
  missing.
- **M2 and M3 (networks).** Each column gets a robust z (median and IQR
  from the fold's fit part), clipped to ±5. A NaN becomes 0, and a
  missing-indicator block is added.
- **Training labels.** T-I labels are winsorized at the fold's fit-part
  0.5% and 99.5% quantiles, for training only. Evaluation always uses the
  raw label.

## Models

### M1: LightGBM, the primary baseline

The research note §5.2 grid, unchanged:

- **Grid, 8 configurations:** `num_leaves` ∈ {15, 63} × `learning_rate` ∈
  {0.02, 0.05} × `min_data_in_leaf` ∈ {200, 1000}.
- **Fixed settings:**
  - `objective` = regression (L2);
  - `feature_fraction` 0.7, `bagging_freq` 1, `lambda_l2` 10,
    `max_bin` 255, `max_depth` −1;
  - `bagging_fraction` 0.7 for T-S1 and 0.3 for T-I. The 24 rows of a
    name-session share one label close and are one observation, not 24;
    subsampling them per tree changes the effective sample very little and
    makes the fit tractable;
  - at most 3,000 rounds, with early stopping after 200 rounds without
    improvement in the validation L2;
  - `deterministic` = true, `force_row_wise` = true, `num_threads` 20.
- **Selection.** Each configuration is fit with seed 0 on the fold's fit
  part and scored on its validation block. The metric is pooled Spearman
  for T-I and the mean daily Spearman IC for T-S1. The best is chosen.
- **Refit.** The chosen configuration is refit on the whole training window
  with rounds = the best iteration × (window rows / fit rows), rounded,
  once for each of seeds 0-4 (`seed`, `bagging_seed`,
  `feature_fraction_seed`). The five are averaged, and per-seed
  predictions are kept.
- **All eight configurations.** Their seed-0 fit-part models also predict
  the test block. Those predictions feed the PBO diagnostic only.
- **Refit schedule.** Every 63 sessions for T-S1 (about 34 fits) and every
  126 sessions for T-I (about 17).

### M2: the Jiang-Kelly-Xiu chart CNN, as published (T-S1 only)

The details were checked against the paper.

- **Images.**
  - I5 is 32×15 and I20 is 64×60, at 3 pixels a day. OHLC is drawn in the
    top four-fifths, with the open tick left, the high-low bar in the
    middle and the close tick right. The moving average (window = image
    length) is one pixel in each day's middle column. Volume bars fill the
    bottom fifth.
  - Each image is scaled to its own window's price extremes (`chart_cnn.
    render_chart`, generalized to 5 days).
- **Network.**
  - Blocks of (5×3 convolution, batch norm, leaky ReLU with slope 0.01,
    2×1 max-pool): 2 blocks (64, 128) for I5, 3 blocks (64, 128, 256) for
    I20.
  - I20's first convolution alone has vertical stride 3 and vertical
    dilation 2. Its padding is set so the fully connected input is the
    published 46,080 (I20) and 15,360 (I5); a unit test pins both sizes.
  - A fully connected layer to 2 classes, with 50% dropout. Xavier
    initialization.
- **Training.**
  - Cross-entropy; Adam at 1e-5, batch 128.
  - Stop when the validation loss has not improved for 2 consecutive
    epochs, at most 100 epochs. Keep the best weights.
  - **Five independently trained networks, averaged.** ŷ is the mean
    probability of class 1.
- **Label.** 1 if r(i, t), the T-S1 relative return, is above the date's
  median. JKX use the sign of the raw return. The median version is the
  same question relative to the hurdle every arm faces, with balanced
  classes.
- **Deviation from the paper, stated.**
  - JKX fit once, on 1993-2000, with a random 70/30 split. Here the fit is
    walk-forward, refit every 252 sessions. Validation is the last 252
    sessions of each training window, because a random split of
    overlapping 20-session labels would leak.
  - There is no grid: I5 and I20 are two trials.

### M3: the level-aware sequence model (T-I and T-S1)

- **Steps.** Each session contributes one gap step and then its 26 bars.
  - T-I uses the six sessions s−5..s (162 steps). The model is causal (left
    padding only) and emits a forecast at each of session s's bar steps
    0..23.
  - T-S1 uses the sixty sessions ending at t (1,620 steps).
- **Channels (12):**
  1. z (a gap step carries g ÷ σ_gap);
  2. ln(C/O_0) ÷ σ_open→k;
  3. ln(C/VWAP_{k−1}) ÷ σ_open→k;
  4. ln(C/PDC) ÷ ATR14;
  5. ln(C/PDH) ÷ ATR14;
  6. ln(C/PDL) ÷ ATR14;
  7. ln(C/EMA21 on 15m) ÷ σ15;
  8. RVOL through k−1;
  9. the name's slot return minus β60 × the side ETF's, ÷ σ_slot;
  10. SPY's slot return ÷ SPY's σ_slot;
  11. the opening-range position (from slot 2), clipped to [−2, 3];
  12. a gap-step flag.

  Undefined values are 0. The volume channels (3 and 8) at bar k use bars
  < k only, as in the intraday block.
- **Architecture.**
  - A learned slot embedding for the 27 step types.
  - A channel-mixing causal TCN: kernel 3, dilations 1, 2, 4, 8 and 16,
    one residual convolution per dilation, receptive field 63 steps, width
    W.
  - A daily branch: an MLP of 2 layers, width W, over the daily block at
    the decision date (T-I: t = s−1; T-S1: t) with its missing-indicator
    block.
  - The head (2 layers):
    - T-I: at each output step, the head reads [TCN output, daily
      branch].
    - T-S1: attention pooling over all steps with a learned query,
      concatenated with the last step and the daily branch.
- **Loss.** MSE on the label, standardized by the fit part's standard
  deviation (T-I, winsorized) or taken as is (T-S1, rank-Gauss).
- **Grid, 8 configurations:** AdamW `lr` ∈ {3e-4, 1e-3} × dropout ∈ {0.1,
  0.3} × W ∈ {32, 64}.
- **Fixed settings:** cosine decay with 5% linear warm-up, weight decay
  1e-2, batch 512, gradient clipping at 1.0, at most 60 epochs, early
  stopping with patience 6 on validation loss, best weights restored.
- **Selection.** Seed 0 per configuration, with the same metric as M1.
  The chosen configuration is then trained with seeds 1-4 as well, and
  the five are averaged. There is no refit on the whole window: the
  early-stopped weights are used, as in JKX.
- **Refit schedule.** Every 252 sessions.

### The walk-forward, for every family

- **Sessions.** The dataset's sorted row dates: sessions s for T-I,
  decision dates t for T-S1.
- **Test blocks.** The first block starts at session index 500. Blocks are
  63, 126 or 252 sessions as above, and the last runs to the end.
- **Training window.** A block starting at index b trains on sessions
  [0, b − gap):
  - T-S1: gap = 26, a 21-session label purge plus a 5-session embargo;
  - T-I: gap = 6, 1 plus 5.
- **Nested validation.** The validation block is the last 252 sessions of
  the training window. The fit part ends a further gap before it. All
  early stopping and configuration choices use only these. Test blocks
  never touch a choice.
- **Determinism.** Seeds are recorded;
  `torch.use_deterministic_algorithms(True)` and
  `cudnn.benchmark = False`.
- **Seed stability.** Per-seed out-of-sample predictions are saved. A
  result whose decision-test mean changes sign in 2 or more of the 5
  single-seed forecasts is not accepted.

## Decision tests

- **T-I.** `market_fill_timing` on the `/4` policy's own orders, filled
  from the SIP cube, over 20 offsets. `<family>_filter` and
  `<family>_free` are each paired against `dip_or_close`, the board's
  rule.
  - The engine's cost is 10 bp. The run is repeated at 16 bp (the live
    slippage the operator reported) and at 25 bp.
  - **Robustness:** a `--next-bar` run moves every fill at a bar close to
    the next bar's open, for candidate and control alike. That is the price
    a person acting after the bar closes can get. A fill at bar 25 goes to
    the official close.
  - Also reported:
    - the share of orders filled before the close;
    - per-fill gain against the official close;
    - candidate minus control in bp per order where the two differ, with
      the t clustered by date;
    - the no-forecast and late-trigger counts;
    - buy and sell sides separately.
- **T-S1.** The overlay under the live executor against `ew-redeploy`,
  over 20 offsets at 10, 16 and 25 bp, with the midcycle ledger's exposure
  reading (`midcycle_ew.Ledger`) beside it.

## Kill criteria, fixed now

**A T-I convention REPLACES `dip_or_close` only if all of these hold:**

1. On the choosing window (first forecast session to 2023-12) it beats
   `dip_or_close` by at least **+2.0 bp a session**, with paired
   Newey-West **t ≥ 2.0** (lag 20) at the median offset. This must hold
   at 10, 16 and 25 bp.
2. It holds, at the same floor, in the `--next-bar` run.
3. It is **not worse on 2024-2026**: the mean paired difference is ≥ 0.
4. Its excess clears a **deflated Sharpe ratio ≥ 0.95 at N = 8**
   (`candidate_stats.deflated_sharpe`, with the across-candidate variance
   of the eight outer candidates' excess Sharpe ratios).
5. It is seed-stable, as above.

Anything else is **RECORD**. A convention that fails the floor but gains
at least 25 bp per differing order with t ≥ 3 is written up as **RECORD:
real but immaterial**. The research note §6.3 shows that the book floor
needs 59-152 bp per order.

**The T-S1 overlay is ADOPT (registered) only if all of these hold:**

1. On the choosing window it beats `ew-redeploy` by at least **+2.0 bp a
   session with t ≥ 2.0 at 25 bp**, and it does so at 10 and 16 bp too.
2. It is **above the control in at least 15 of 20 offsets**.
3. Its median worst drawdown is within 3 points of the control's.
4. It is **not negative on 2024-2026**.
5. It clears the deflated Sharpe and seed-stability conditions above.

Otherwise it is RECORD.

A REPLACES or ADOPT verdict changes nothing by itself. The live path is
frozen at `checkpoint-2026-09-28`. A passing candidate becomes a separate,
registered live change only with the operator's go-ahead. For T-I that
change also needs:

- the intraday features computed live every fifteen minutes (IEX price,
  SIP bars one slot late);
- a model artifact;
- a parity test between the live row and the training row.

## Prior, stated before the run

The information that has moved this book so far came from grades,
filings and release tone, not from charts. Priors:

| Outcome | Probability |
|---|---|
| A T-I convention passes | about 3% per family |
| The T-S1 overlay passes (any family) | about 10% |
| Any pass | 12-15% |

- **T-I.** Intraday large-cap direction has lasted minutes in every
  published study. The per-order gain the book floor needs is a third to
  two thirds of a session's standard deviation.
- **T-S1.** The incremental 20-session IC needed is about 0.03-0.04. Trees
  on technicals reached 0.010-0.019.

The likeliest outcome is RECORD everywhere, with the feature importances
saying what the models used.

**What would make the prior wrong.**

- A T-I model with an out-of-sample pooled IC above 0.03 that survives the
  partial-IC control, and a `free` convention whose per-order gain rises
  with |ŷ|.
- A T-S1 model whose daily IC is above 0.03 in both halves of 2016-2023,
  with the gain spread across offsets.

Either would be the first evidence on this book that the chart carries
information the grade does not.

## Order of work and compute

1. **Features.** `stage3_features` (daily and intraday blocks) with the
   causality tests:
   - tamper with session t+1 and assert that the rows of t are unchanged;
   - tamper with bar k's high, low and volume and with every field of bars
     after k, and assert that the row at slot k is unchanged.
2. **Export on spark1** (store-bound). Then transfer spark1 → Cowork VM →
   RTX, with a sha256 check at each hop.
3. **M1 on the RTX desktop's CPU** (i9-10900K, 20 threads). T-S1 comes
   first.
4. **Decision tests on spark1** (store-bound).
5. **M3 and M2 on the RTX 5080**, regardless of M1's outcome. This is the
   fair test of the operator's claim about networks with the right inputs.
6. **Write-up:** one verdict per candidate.

What goes on in parallel:

- the market-hours rule: no deploys between 09:00 and 16:15 ET, and none
  of this touches the live path;
- the Tuesday 19:30 nightly, whose redeploy leg is checked read-only.

**Deviations from the research note's proposal, all decided here:**

- T-E1, T-X1 and T-E3 are folded into T-I, and T-S3 is deferred.
- M2's intraday-image extension is dropped: M3 covers the intraday path.
- T-I uses `bagging_fraction` 0.3 and refits every 126 sessions.
- Networks use early-stopped weights without a full-window refit.
- Configurations are chosen on validation IC rather than on a validation
  decision value.
- PBO and SPA are reported rather than gated. The deflated Sharpe at
  N = 8 is the gate.
- Model decisions are judged from the first forecast session rather than
  from 2016.

## Addendum before any run (2026-09-29, 06:10Z)

These were written after the code was built and before any stage-3 model
had been trained or scored, so none of them is tuning.

**One amendment: M3 gets a learned session-position embedding.**

- It is an `Embedding(K, W)`, with K = 6 for T-I and K = 60 for T-S1.
  Row p is added to the input projection of every step of the window's
  session p, where 0 is the oldest session and K−1 is the row's own. It
  sits beside the step-type embedding and is initialized N(0, 0.02²).
- The research proposal's M3 had "learned slot and session embeddings".
  This note's text dropped the session half.
- Without it, T-S1's attention pooling cannot tell which of the 60
  sessions a pattern came from, beyond the last 63 steps. That is the same
  blind spot §0.1 of the research note criticizes in stage 2.
- A test shows the model with the embedding learns a label that depends on
  which session a spike sits in (out-of-sample IC +0.60). Without the
  embedding it cannot (+0.01).

**What the build decided where this note was silent.** The code, its
docstrings and each forecast's meta record these. They are fixed from now
on.

- **M1:**
  - The bin edges come from the labelled rows of the training window, so
    from the fit and validation features, never the test rows. LightGBM
    samples its default 200,000 rows, deterministically.
  - `feature_pre_filter` is false.
  - Early stopping uses the validation labels winsorized with the fit
    part's bounds, as part of training. The configuration is chosen on the
    raw labels.
  - The refit rounds are rounded half to even.
- **M3:**
  - GELU activations, in pre-LayerNorm residual blocks
    `x + dropout(GELU(conv(LayerNorm(x))))`.
  - The robust z divides by the IQR.
  - T-I targets are scaled by the fit part's standard deviation of the
    winsorized labels, and not centered.
  - The warm-up is 5% of the planned optimizer steps.
  - Seed 0 of the chosen configuration is the grid's own network.
  - With a 63-step receptive field, sessions s−5 and s−4 of the T-I
    window never reach a forecast. They are registered as they are, and a
    test pins the fact.
- **M2:**
  - I20's first convolution is padded (7, 1), which gives the published
    46,080 inputs to the fully connected layer.
  - The label is "strictly above the date's median".
  - A missing moving-average pixel leaves the image in and is counted.
  - Early folds are small: fold 0 has 196 fit sessions. Their `epochs` and
    `best_epoch` are reported beside any null result.
- **Decision tests:**
  - **Forecast values.**
    - A forecast of exactly 0 at the trigger vetoes the dip or pop fill.
    - A missing forecast at the trigger slot, inside a vector that has
      others, leaves the board's fill standing. Such vectors are counted.
  - **The model window.** It runs from each family's first forecast
    session to 2023-12-29.
  - **Costs.** The +2 bp floor must hold at 10, 16 and 25 bp. Every other
    criterion is read at 25 bp and reported at the others.
  - **Deflated Sharpe.** The candidates' Sharpe variance includes the
    candidate's own Sharpe, which is conservative.
  - **Seed stability.** It needs all five seed runs. A seed mean that is
    missing or zero counts as a sign change.
  - **Scope of `--next-bar`.** It moves every bar-close fill:
    - `dip_or_close`;
    - the four T-I conventions;
    - the dip-rule level conventions;
    - the two support/resistance conventions.
- **The overlay.** It changes the allocator's targets only, as registered.
  - The live executor's mid-cycle breakout entry does not read those
    targets. A dropped name can therefore be bought back between resets
    when cash is on hand.
  - This is measured and reported (`drops.held_after_reset_drop`), not
    changed. Gating entries on the overlay would be a new trial.

## Implementation record before any run (2026-09-29, 06:25Z)

These are the definitions the feature code (`stage3_features.py`,
`stage3_intraday.py`, `stage3_export.py`) chose inside the catalogue's
wording. They were fixed before any dataset was exported. The tests pin
the no-look-ahead rule for every column:

- **Daily block.** Every input is tampered with after row t, and every
  column's rows up to t must be unchanged.
- **Intraday block.** Bar k's high, low and volume, and every field of
  later bars, are tampered with. This includes the levels in force and
  the breadth, and the row at slot k must be unchanged.

**Daily block**

- **Windows.** They are strict, and a window with a missing value gives
  NaN.
- **D04.** Slopes are raw log changes.
- **D05.** %b is omitted because it is linear in z. The squeeze
  percentile ranks today's bandwidth among the trailing 126, today
  included.
- **D08.** The breakout is a close above the highest high of the prior n
  sessions.
- **D09.** "Since the 52-week high" counts sessions since the last time
  the window's high was set.
- **D14.** Weeks end on Friday, or on the last session before a Friday
  holiday. Months end on their last session. A period ending at t's
  close counts at t.
- **D15.** Residual 12-1 momentum regresses on SPY over t−251..t−21, and
  the residuals' sum is divided by their standard deviation.
- **D16.**
  - Shares are the latest EDGAR `shares` fact filed before the session.
  - Weekly turnover is clipped to [0, 1].
  - The reference price uses weeks w, w−1, … and CGO_w reads
    reference[w−2] against P_{w−2}.
- **D17.** It uses 0.25% log-price bins (the `sr_levels` volume-profile
  bins, not 0.25·ATR). The value area grows from the point of control
  toward the fuller neighbour. Each session's bars are scaled by its own
  official close, which is known at t.
- **D18.** step = 10^(⌊log10 P⌋ − 1), with P the cube's raw official
  close.
- **D20.** The earnings anchor is the strict publication session of the
  last 8-K 2.02. The 52-week anchors are the last session setting the
  window's high and low. The daily VWAP is the typical price weighted by
  volume, from the anchor through t.
- **DS.** Levels come from `sr_levels.daily_levels` row t+1. The panel's
  last row is NaN.
- **M01.** Side ETF returns come from cubes, with early closes as zero.
- **M02.** The theme basket is the leave-one-out equal weight of the
  name's primary theme.
- **M03.** Weights are the positive part of the 252-session correlation
  within the primary theme.
- **M06.** abvol5 = ln(mean V over t−4..t / mean V over t−54..t−5).
- **M07.** The news is the earnings reaction sessions (strict
  publication).
- **V01-V03.**
  - The overnight gap counts as a return in RV.
  - The HAR means are logs of the mean RV.
  - Realized skewness covers the last 5 sessions' 27 returns each.
- **X.** EDGAR uses `strict_publication=True`, tone uses
  `strict_before_session=True`, and X05 is zero on earnings sessions.
- **G.** Bullish means a stance above 0. Grade age and flips count the
  desk's grade changes.
- **C.** Weekday one-hot plus the 13 calendar columns, all date-level.

**Intraday block**

- **Rows.** Name-sessions whose previous panel session t had the name as
  an eligible, graded member. S1 rows start at the first SIP session,
  2016-01-04.
- **Daily prices.** All intraday prices are on the session's raw basis.
  Daily levels are moved there by adj_close[t] / prior_close[s].
- **I05.** The VWAP is the typical price; the IS block's VWAP level is
  `sr_levels`' close-weighted proxy.
- **I11.** 15-minute EMAs and RSI run over the name's consecutive cube
  closes, after a 3-session warm-up.
- **IT.** Hourly closes are slots 3, 7, 11, 15, 19 and 23 and the
  session's last bar. Before 10:30 the previous session's last hour
  stands.
- **V06.** The two forecast columns are in the T-I rows only.

**Sequence tensor.** The gap step carries:

- the gap in σ_gap units;
- the open against the prior-day levels, in ATR units;
- the side-relative and SPY gaps.

It carries 0 in the since-open, VWAP, EMA, RVOL and opening-range
channels.
