# Entry training population correction

Registered after the all-stock rule-relative candidate was independently
verified, before this variant's fits or economic outcomes. The all-stock
candidate failed superiority: -16.3362pp median paired gain, 6/20 phases win.
Its 104 numeric monthly models / 4,081,392 predictions and twenty saved books
(17,445 intents) were verified. No phase or threshold was selected from them.

Hypothesis: fitting expected entry advantage across unbuyable stocks dilutes
the conditional objective of the actual strategy. Restrict training rows to
original prior_eligible true and prior_grades >=2, exactly the original grade
selection condition, while excluding SPY/QQQ as before. This uses the already
authenticated prior-session arrays, never the current/future grade.

Change nothing else: same target, 21 features, model configuration, clocks,
monthly maturity/freeze, all20 zero-cost planning phases, fixed periods,
sell gate, selection/sizing and missing-data behavior. This is a separately
named exploratory variant, not a repair that erases the first result.
Report every phase and preserve models/forecasts/ledgers. No live promotion
without consistent evidence; no extra threshold/hyperparameter search.
