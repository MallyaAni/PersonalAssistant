# Stage 5: buying at the decision day's close: pre-registration (2026-09-29)

## The question

The desk decides after the close, and the board buys in the next session.
Should the book's buys instead fill at the decision day's close, from a
decision made at 15:30 ET on 15:30 data?

The operator asked whether the models lost "because at the open, within the
first 15 minutes, most of the move has already happened".
[The stage-4 follow-up](stage4-audit-and-overnight-2026-09-29.md) found that
it happens overnight, not in the first 15 minutes. After a buy decision:

- the price rose +14.6 bp from the decision close to the next open in
  2018-2023, and +44.1 bp in 2024-2026 (per buy, submitted basis);
- the board's fill was +22.3 and +51.9 bp above the decision close.

Sells went the other way, so they stay on the next session.

## What is known before this note (disclosed)

- **The diagnostic above.** It measured, on stage 4's orders from
  2017-12-27, essentially the quantity this study tests: the decision close
  against the board's fill. **2018-2026 has therefore been seen.**
- **The literature memo**
  ([stage5-lit-overnight-2026-09-29.md](stage5-lit-overnight-2026-09-29.md)).
  - The overnight premium in large caps is old and documented.
  - Weighted by order size, the diagnostic's gain is smaller: +0.8 to +1.0
    bp a session on 2018-2023 and +0.2 to +0.9 on 2024-2026, gross.
  - About a third or more of it is one session of the names' drift, that
    is, being invested a session earlier.
  - Its point that nightly grade changes cannot be known at 15:45 is
    superseded by the measurement below.
- **The timing audit**
  ([stage5-timing-audit-2026-09-29.md](stage5-timing-audit-2026-09-29.md)).
  It compared decisions only; no fill or return was computed.
  - About 98% of buy notional is decided identically at 15:45 as at 19:30
    (98.3% and 96.2% at 15:30, on 2018-2023 and 2024-2026).
  - The grade's three-session persistence settles it by the afternoon.
  - It built and null-tested the one-step proxy that this study uses.
- **2016-01-04 to 2017-12-26 has not been examined** for this quantity:
  423 executed buys at offset 10.

## The decision

- **Policy.** `graded-equal-weight/5`, the 25% cap live from 2026-09-30,
  under the `ew-redeploy` executor. Stage 4's control, with the `/5`
  allocator.
- **Control.** Every buy fills by the board's `dip_or_close` in session
  t+1, exactly as in stage 4 (`stage4_labels` control, adjusted basis via
  `fill_timing.session_scale`).
- **Candidate.** Every eligible buy is decided from the 15:30 proxy of
  session t and fills at t's official close: the auction print, else the
  last bar, times the session scale, which equals `adj_close[t]`.
  - **Eligible:** reset buys, deferred retries, rotation buys, band entries
    and redeploy buys.
  - **Not eligible, filling as the control:** FOMC restorations (next-open
    by policy), every buy on a 13:00 early-close session, and names with no
    15:15-15:30 bar.
- **Sells** are decided at 19:30 on the final data and fill as the control.
- **The 15:30 proxy** is the timing audit's §3 method, fixed now. It
  replaces row t with:
  - the close of the 15:15-15:30 bar (slot 23), and the high and low of
    slots 0-23 with the day's open;
  - SIP volume through 15:15, scaled by the name's trailing-20 median ratio
    of full-day to that volume;
  - filings strictly before t, and tone with a 15:30 acceptance cutoff;
  - the expectations gap at t−1;
  - one step of stance persistence, the rotation gate and the grade rule;
  - the executor's blocker and band z on the partial bar;
  - the planner on the book at t.

  **The null test comes first.** Row t set to the final row must reproduce
  the report and the control journal exactly. It is registered and run
  before any fill is read.
- **Why 15:30.** A person acting on the board needs time before the 15:50
  market-on-close cutoff. 15:45 is reported beside it and decides nothing.
- **Where the two decisions differ**, per (session, name):
  - **Matched** (the smaller of the two sizes): the candidate fill against
    the control fill.
  - **Missed** (19:30 wants more): it fills as the control, g = 0.
  - **Extra** (15:30 bought what 19:30 does not want): a round trip, bought
    at t's close and sold in t+1 by the board's sell rule, charged 25 bp
    each way.

## The measurement

Stage 4's machinery is used throughout.

- **Orders.** The control executor on the executed basis at 20 start
  offsets, read at the median offset (10).
- **Per buy.** g = `stage4_labels.gain`(control, candidate, "buy") in bp. The
  t is clustered by decision date.
- **The book.** Σ weight × g per session, in bp of equity, with a
  Newey-West t at lag 20. Extra round trips enter the session's bp.
- **Robustness, reported:**
  - the control at `next_open` (the paper account's convention);
  - the 15:45 decision;
  - a full ledger walk of the candidate executor;
  - by kind (reset, retry, rotation, band entry, redeploy);
  - the drift-adjusted reading (the A/A+ drift of one session per buy).

## Windows

- **Deciding:** 2016-01-04 to 2017-12-26, which was not examined.
- **Replication:** 2018-2023 and 2024-2026. They were seen, so they can only
  veto.
- **Forward:** a no-trade shadow of the 15:30 decisions from the first
  session after the build.

## Criteria, fixed now

**PASS** if all of these hold:

1. On the deciding window, the mean g per eligible buy is above zero with a
   date-clustered t ≥ 2.0, and the session mean is above zero.
2. On 2018-2023 and on 2024-2026, the mean g per eligible buy is above zero.
3. On the deciding window, the mean g against the `next_open` control is
   above zero.
4. The null test passes.

Anything else is **RECORD**.

**What a PASS does.**

- It lets the change be proposed to the operator: a BUY for the close at
  about 15:30, and the paper account's market-on-close buys.
- A live change is its own release and needs his go-ahead in chat. It also
  depends on his broker taking market-on-close orders: Robinhood does not,
  Schwab and IBKR do.
- The forward shadow keeps running. At 250 forward buys a per-buy t ≤ −2.0
  reverts the change.

## Trials and prior

- **Trials.** One registered variant. The cumulative count goes from 451
  to 452.
- **Prior.**
  - About 30% for a PASS. If the effect is real, about +20 bp a buy, the
    deciding window has roughly even odds of t ≥ 2 on 423 buys. The
    literature puts the effect's existence at better than even, but much of
    it is drift.
  - About 10% that the gain, if adopted, reaches +1 bp a session of equity.
