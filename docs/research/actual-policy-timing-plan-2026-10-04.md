# Actual-policy timing comparison

Freeze this specification before producing any new economic accounts.

The question is whether stock-specific frozen execution distributions improve
the current policy's funded cumulative wealth when selection, allocation and
holding exits remain unchanged. The earlier periodic fractional-share
component accounts do not answer this question. This comparison uses the real
nightly planner, intraday sender, pending state, retry and settlement paths.

Evaluate the unchanged rule, saved Boosting CDF and saved Ridge CDF at 0, 10
and 25 basis points per side. Each arm starts with $100,000 cash, no holdings
and zero cash yield on each of the first twenty exchange sessions beginning
2018-02-01. Each book carries actual shares, cash, receivables and the actual
nightly rebalance clock through 2026-09-30. This is 180 new policy accounts;
overlapping start dates are sensitivity checks, not independent trials. No
phase, model, cost, date or parameter is selected after inspecting outcomes.

Keep graded-equal-weight/5 selection and sizing, grade rotations, band entry
and rejection rules, cash redeployment and FOMC event handling unchanged.
Only ordinary pre-deadline execution timing differs. Both CDFs retain their
original one-decision horizon, fitted bytes, calibration, support and freeze;
no model is fitted or calibrated again. Missing distributions wait, without
an implicit 1% fallback. The sender's actual terminal clock remains shared.
The candidate is not a learned holding/profit-exit policy. That and adaptive
position sizing require separate predeclared comparisons after this result.

Use the original 96-symbol snapshot and original SIP cubes, authenticated by
their existing byte receipts. Daily archives are split adjusted; cubes are
raw per-session dollars. Nightly report prefixes use the entire past daily
history in that night's share basis, not discontinuous historical raw-share
prices. Execution and broker accounting use raw session dollars. Reverse
archive adjustment only with dated split records; do not fit price ratios.
Dividend amounts require an explicit source-unit declaration. Converted cash
amounts and original values remain separate. Unknown payment dates produce
unspendable receivables, never invented funding. Same-day split/dividend
ordering and all-name action declarations not authenticated remain explicit
limitations; affected economic attribution must not be presented as proven.

The current report contains declared eligible, known-grade names plus SPY;
QQQ is never a selection candidate. Missing grades are not replaced with A
or C. Excluded names and reasons remain recorded. Unrepresented held names
retain broker holdings and raw marks; do not add their prices to the planner
and accidentally fabricate liquidation. Grades, membership and daily history
are current-vintage supplied evidence, not exact historical live availability.
Unavailable analyst votes/convictions must not masquerade as model inputs.

At each completed regular 15-minute bar, introduce only that completed close,
its bar start and the session open observed by then. All requests in the
batch see the same observation; later fill prices enter the private broker
only after the entire batch has been decided. Price ordinary market requests
at the next consecutive bar's original raw open. This ideal boundary-time
proxy proves neither execution latency, midpoint fills nor broker fills.
The 15:45 terminal decision uses the 15:45 bar open, not the closing auction.
Queued next-open event requests use the explicit session opening proxy;
closing-auction requests require the separate original auction print. Missing
prices are unavailable outcomes, with no close substitution. Original cubes
exclude early closes; retain those sessions and actual clock boundaries as
unavailable intraday evidence. No interpolated bars or fabricated auction.

Persist every planned intent, observed wait/unavailability, refusal, retry,
accepted request, partial/missing fill, cancellation, pending end-of-sample
leg, held-mark gap and skipped nightly. Original historical decision clocks
are distinct from new artifact creation/publication clocks. Failed or unknown
NAV marks remain missing; they cannot be forward-filled or removed to improve
reported results. Sale proceeds from a future fill batch cannot fund that
batch's buys, but a subsequent observation may use already realized cash.
Submitted orders remain whole-share quantities; split entitlements can be
fractional. Preserve the actual sender/planner behavior and disclose where
the hypothetical venue differs from broker corporate-action processing.

For each start and cost, include SPY and QQQ buy-and-hold controls with matching
raw dollars, whole-share capital, costs, dividend treatment, dates and marks.
Do not reuse the earlier fractional total-return benchmark as if equivalent.
Report funded cumulative gain as the primary objective, paired candidate-minus-
rule gain, CAGR, Sharpe, worst drawdown, fees, realized turnover, exposure,
missed/refused quantities, and common paired completeness. Report all starts,
the median/range and count better, not only a best start. Retain fixed slices
2018-2020, 2021-2026 and the previously reused 2026-08-17 onward window. Models
lack mature support for a 2016 start; do not silently backfill predictions or
claim the requested 2016-2020 slice was tested in full. Recent data has already
been examined and is not an untouched test set. No unreliable significance
claim from overlapping books. Price-component and receivable wealth must be
distinguishable where payment completeness limits total-return interpretation.

Before outcomes, require actual synthetic planner/sender/persistence journeys,
future-prefix invariance, original-symbol lookup, cash/coverage and corporate-
action accounting tests, raw-input preflight and a pinned immutable source.
An independent verifier must check saved accounts and arithmetic without
calling the producer or resimulating accounts. No old studies, source audits,
fits, model gates or frozen histories are repeated. No real orders, paid data,
production UI/model changes or strategy adoption occur in this research step.

Adoption requires evidence of a material and reasonably consistent funded
improvement over the current rule across the declared starts, cost levels and
eras, assessed with drawdown/turnover and missingness rather than return alone.
Benchmark outperformance inherited from selection is not evidence for timing.
If evidence remains weak, preserve the live rule and continue developing the
remaining-horizon volatility/structure and holding-exit models. Never convert
a weak comparison into a superiority claim by changing its objective.
