# Saved holding-distribution diagnostics

Objective: measure predictive distributions before using their probabilities for
joint funded sizing/retention. This does not fit, tune, trade or prove advantage.
VERIFIED: source1500cc4b, original104 numeric heads and172170 expanded predictions;
92/97 fixed final-date requests have joint support. UNVERIFIED: conditional
calibration and funded wealth advantage. Keep the exact original producer,
joint protocol and saved risk/parent/bridge/input bytes unchanged.

Freeze before new diagnostic outcomes: all94 nonbenchmark stocks at every
completed session from2018-02-01 through2026-09-30, including unavailable
predictions and outcomes. Primary reporting window2026-08-17..09-30;
secondary all,2018–20,2021–26. The recent window was reused in preceding
research and is not pristine holdout. Nothing is selected from these results.
Use existing252 joint dates/756-session banks, monthly purge and August17
freeze. Do not refit, restore/run an estimator, regenerate forecasts, replay
an account or repeat any completed control study.

Request each singleton distribution before reading its current label. Report
prediction availability separately from label maturity/missingness. Endpoint
publication uses actual New York exchange close, including early closes.
When D+2 lies outside the supplied array, the reviewed exchange calendar
distinguishes a genuinely future label from a past missing outcome. Neither
can change prediction admission. Retain every requested stock/session.

For each available distribution with a known original one-session arithmetic
label, score positive hypothetical round-trip net gain at0/10/25bp per side:
net(r,c)=(1+r)*(1-c)/(1+c)-1. This is a single-stock price-component diagnostic,
not an actual funded fee, broker fill, dividend/corporate-action accounting or
portfolio return. Strictly positive means gain; zero is not a winning outcome.

Report Brier score, CRPS, predicted/realized mean and mean error, inverse
weighted empirical10/50/90th quantiles,80% interval coverage/width and PIT
left/right brackets. Compute CRPS by sorted cumulative weights, independently
checked against its pairwise definition. Accept only possible returns >=-1
and finite positive probabilities summing to one within1e-12; normalize only
floating sum roundoff for metric arithmetic, without changing relative weights.
Do not repair labels, clip tails, drop sample rows or manufacture missing risk.

Reference distribution: original realized labels from the exact same strictly
mature bank dates, same equal probabilities, without current model conditioning.
It is a past-only stock-specific empirical baseline, not a fitted challenger.
Compare both proper scores on identical available/known cases. Do not score a
reference on additional cases and present unequal denominators as superiority.

Publish common counts/proper scores/ten equal-width probability reliability bins
([0,.1),...,[.9,1]) for each fixed window/cost,
each stock, original training/trading-support versus outside-original-support,
and causal volatility context. Stock-volatility thirds use each decision's
original20-session volatility ranks among price-supported nonbenchmark stocks,
independent of outcome availability; ties use the empirical midpoint rank.
Market-volatility thirds compare current
SPY20-session volatility to at least252 finite historical values in the756
sessions before that month's first session, cutoff strictly before min(month,
August17). Missing context remains an explicit group. These are diagnostic
strata, not exposure rules, selected regimes or assertions about the future.

No significance claim from correlated overlapping stock/session cases or a
short reused window. Label units remain adjusted-open proxies/current-vintage
inputs; forecast quality is not profitability. Record all missing requests,
source/artifact hashes, untouched original files and exact consumer code.
Save compact per-case numeric/status evidence and independently recompute proper
scores and aggregates without fitting or account replay. A poor metric leads
to a targeted declared investigation, not window/threshold/model mining.

Acceptance before saved-data evaluation: weighted two-point CRPS versus
pairwise formula; correct costs/ties/total loss/domain refusal; empirical
quantiles/PIT; actual saved-head reader path with no grade permission;
prediction requests retained for missing/immature labels; unknown endpoints
and delayed past labels; same-date sample provenance and no future labels in
reference; original producer files unchanged. Then one authenticated diagnostic
run and independent artifact verification. Funded compounded gain, SPY/QQQ,
live parity and guarded deployment remain required separate acceptance paths.
