# Forward monthly model publication

This is a shadow infrastructure continuation, not a new model or policy adoption.
The fixed held-B arithmetic head retains thirteen completed-close features,
the registered 64-tree estimator, equal weight per training date, 504-day minimum,
756-session window and strict label endpoint before the lesser of the monthly
fit session and 2026-08-17. Monthly fit date is the first reviewed exchange
session; omitted sessions, unknown calendar years and unfinished input closes
are refused. Original artifact authentication belongs to the supplying caller.

Publication records the writer's actual timezone-aware clock after numeric
serialization (an injected clock is only for synthetic acceptance). It must follow the first
fit-session close and remain within that month. A late publication cannot be used
earlier, and a September head cannot serve an October closing decision. An
explicit September closing observation can remain valid until its following
opening across midnight or month-end. Numeric arrays
are saved without pickle; exact receipt and array byte hashes are required on
reload. Existing artifacts cannot be overwritten. Missing training remains an
explicit unavailable publication, with no fabricated model.

Acceptance: same selected training rows and numeric predictions as the existing
held-B monthly path; frozen/mature labels; publication-time and month boundaries;
calendar gaps; unfinished closes; byte tampering; unavailable training and fresh
output.

Current close continuation reuses the exact full-prefix thirteen-feature helper,
including completed-close benchmark support. It requires an actual input clock,
the original symbol order, a complete reviewed calendar and a fresh close whose
next opening has not passed. Model and close receipts retain separate publication
clocks; numeric inference is recomputed on reload. The close artifact stores the
consumed price/grade prefix so a filtered nightly report and explicit mechanical
share conversion can be checked exactly, without estimating a price ratio.

Forward risk retains the original OOS simultaneous dates, strict maturity/freeze,
756-session bank window, 252 common-date minimum, uniform date mass and the same
mean-preserving stock-volatility transform. Missing required stocks, history or
volatility remain unavailable. Only the existing private funded planner accepts
this reader; its broker guard still requires ReplayBroker and the exact decision
clock, now also checking forecast expiry. No real orders, production selector or
strategy promotion is enabled. Actual current-source/shadow evidence and the
completed funded comparison remain required before live selection.

Saved-risk admission stays strict by default. An explicit `allow_saved_origin`
option admits only the exact original three-file producer source map registered
in `direct_feature_arithmetic.SAVED_RISK_SOURCE`. Numeric export was added to the
direct module after that artifact was produced; no inference formula changed.
Original and consumer source maps remain separate in the reader receipt, and
all original parent, input, support, monthly forecast and price hashes are still
required. Changed producer hashes or forecasts refuse even with the option.
