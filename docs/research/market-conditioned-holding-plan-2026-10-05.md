# Market-conditioned holding calibration

Objective: condition stock-specific holding forecasts on the observed market
without selecting a fixed bullish/bearish gate or dip/profit percentage.
This advances the authorized learned exposure component. V3's first zero-cost
account and causal attribution are already observed development evidence:
26.16% CAGR versus the rule's28.34%, with48.97% versus43.09% maximum loss.
Its positive calm/rising contribution does not establish a tradable switch.

Register one design before its own fitted economic outcomes. All existing
accounts, producers, models and source snapshots remain unchanged. Initial
checkpoint is the numerical fitter and acceptance; reader/funded integration,
saved-data diagnostics, portfolio comparison and prospective evidence are
required subsequent work, not implied by a passing numeric test.

Use the existing authenticated completed-close feature prefix. Fixed context:
SPY20-session log return, SPY252-session drawdown, SPY20-session volatility,
and original20-session breadth. Retain continuous values, with no categorical
market threshold, fitted sign restriction, new window search or alternate
feature menu. The existing stock forecast remains a separate predictor.

For each stock/month, fit non-default log return by ordinary least squares on
intercept, original log-gross forecast and those four market features. Preserve
the original preceding756 exchange-session maximum,252 admitted-date minimum,
strict D+2 outcome maturity, monthly opening cutoff and August17 freeze.
The fitter receives dates and outcome endpoints and enforces selection itself;
arrays supplied without dates cannot be called an honest walk-forward fit.
No current/future label or feature affects the earlier fitted coefficients.
Missing features on an otherwise admitted training row cause explicit refusal,
not silent removal or imputation. Record selected rows and all input hashes.

Center/scale each predictor using only selected non-default training rows.
Use SVD with the standard machine-precision numerical rank rule. Retain a
positive residual degree of freedom. Refuse current queries outside the
identified training affine span rather than fabricating precision in an
unidentified direction. This is a numerical identifiability rule, not a price
threshold. Report the conditional log mean and regression prediction-leverage
adjustment; neither is a calibrated probability guarantee.

Later scenario integration must keep exact simultaneous residual dates,
original actual -1 default rows, stock-specific current/prior volatility,
equal empirical date probabilities, overflow refusal and original provenance.
Use the same transformation for historical and published forward observations.
The existing jointly certified net-log-growth optimizer will translate those
conditional scenarios into cash, whole-share additions and discretionary exits.
No production policy or new economic account grid is registered in this step.

Acceptance: independently solved raw design regression and leverage; causal
calendar/month/freeze selection; future-label/feature prefix invariance;
true defaults; stock-context sensitivity; constant/collinear design and
out-of-span refusal; numeric tails, missing/domain/type errors and immutable
caller buffers. Subsequent acceptance must exercise actual private planning,
publication, funding, timing, fills and saved arithmetic before scoring.

Complete the original three-cost V3 screen first. A subsequent economic run
needs a separately fixed identity, identical original inputs/costs/start,
incumbent/SPY/QQQ controls and explicit missingness. Primary objective remains
full compounded net gain, with drawdown, Sharpe, exposure and turnover reported;
favorable eras/regimes cannot replace that objective. Reused development history
and regression assumptions cannot justify live adoption on their own.
