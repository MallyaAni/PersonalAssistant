# Recorded Dip versus incumbent price-entry evidence

This prospective extension stores original inputs for a later fair comparison;
it does not select a new live policy. Existing records without the extension
remain missing evidence. Never populate them using today's code or data.

For every successfully collected completed 15-minute research observation,
capture the current grade, original price setup, Bollinger position, 21-day EMA
stretch, and exact 20 adjusted close inputs/dates used for the band. Preserve
the observation time, bar start, expiry, price basis and source fingerprints.
Keep raw setup separate from common blockers: grade below the existing entry
floor, FOMC/event pause, stale nightly decision, rejecting upper band, or
unavailable price features. Reject incomplete, expired, misaligned and
out-of-session bars, including bars after an early close.

The incumbent price opinion calls the production `entry_action` with the
current grade and a hypothetical flat position. The challenger uses the
existing recorded Dip definition, under the same common blockers. These are
unfunded price-rule opinions. Neither establishes personal cash, position caps,
an executable quote, an actual recommendation displayed to a person, or a fill.
No broker or personal holding is written. A+ remains a grade, not entry timing.

Later comparison must retain a common ticker/session opportunity denominator,
include absent signals and failed dips, deduplicate repeated readings, and
group policy identities separately. Earliest qualifying signals must precede
their execution proxies. Keep the existing 5-session and 20-session horizons,
report missing/immature outcomes, and stress costs at 0/10/25 basis points.
Do not equate next-observed prices with midpoint fills, tune after outcomes,
or promote from a few stocks in one market day. A signal-return comparison
does not establish funded portfolio performance or exact personal-account
reconstruction. Its calendar folds and regime labels must use information
available at their decision times.

Acceptance covers distinct Dip/breakout cases, common blocking with setup
preserved, reproduction of the band from recorded inputs, invalid bars and
early close, real builder integration, immutable repeat publication, legacy
records left unchanged, and independence from account holdings/cash.
