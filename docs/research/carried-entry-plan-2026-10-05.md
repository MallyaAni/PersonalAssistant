# Carried entry diagnostic

Objective: determine whether the same-session buy deadline masks useful timing
opportunities. This is a research component test, not a production promotion.

Before examining results, fix one intervention: unattempted buys remain active
until the next existing twenty-session plan replaces them. Preserve original
target quantities, original unspent cash budget, sell timing, and the session's
1% crossing. Do not force a final-day buy or recycle sale proceeds. A selected
attempt is consumed even if its subsequent execution price is missing. Never
choose an attempt from that future price. Partial fills retain the incumbent's
one-attempt convention. Early closes remain unsupported in both arms.

Evaluate all twenty phases at zero costs on the original authenticated inputs.
Require the new engine's no-carry arm to match every original control daily
curve exactly before interpreting results. Record every plan and attempted fill,
including its actual later session; retain unfilled and superseded intentions.
Report full, early, later and reused recent windows without selecting a winning
phase. Favorable results would justify further investigation, not live adoption:
this does not reproduce the live daily planner's replacement permissions, and
the recent historical window has already been examined in other experiments.

Acceptance for this diagnostic: real-engine lifetime tests, exact original
control parity, funded cash/share/wealth reconciliation, and an independent
saved-ledger reconstruction. No parameter search or model fitting is involved.
