---
name: Check-ins
description: Being asked later how something went - a trip, an interview, a hard week - or checked on after it. Off unless the person asks. Turn it on or off for them, set one for one thing they name, or say what is waiting.
---
The person is asking about check-ins: the assistant coming back later, on its own, to ask how something went or how they are. Use manage_check_ins for what they asked: mode "on" when they want that from now on for the things they mention; "off" when they want it to stop; "once" with the subject, the question in their own words, and how many days out, when they name one thing to be asked about later; "status" when they ask what is waiting.

Then say plainly what is now set, in one or two warm lines: when you'll ask and about what, or that check-ins are on or off. Do not call it a task, a reminder, or an automation, and do not list rules.
